# Explosion intelligence: build and evaluation report

Generated by `build_explosion.py`. All numbers below are computed from the files, not estimated.

## 1. Data import and validation

- Imported 200 synthetic scenarios from explosion_specific_mining_scenarios_200.xlsx; IDs unique; 0 duplicate texts; categories match Category_Summary.
- All rows marked synthetic and Historical_DGMS_Record = No. No source values were changed.
- Event wording heuristic: 139 scenarios describe an explosion/ignition event, 61 describe a potential hazard only.
- Mine-type context per category: Blasting and Explosives-Related Explosion: Coal or Metal; Coal-Dust Explosion: Coal; Combined Methane and Coal-Dust Explosion: Coal; Electrical or Mechanical Ignition of Explosive Atmosphere: Coal or Metal; Metal Mine: Gas, Dust and Other Explosion Mechanisms: Metal; Methane / Firedamp Explosion: Coal; Spontaneous Heating, Fire and Explosion Escalation: Primarily Coal.
- Labels are the workbook's *suggested* categories; the Data_Dictionary says expert validation is required. Some overlap is visible, for example scenarios labelled Coal-Dust that describe a methane ignition raising dust, and Metal Mine scenarios that describe blasting events.

## 2. Historical DGMS records mapped to the taxonomy

- Historical records searched: 610; core explosion events (from the project's DGMS coding): 31.
- A record gets a taxonomy category only when its DGMS-derived explosion label supports it:

  - Blasting and Explosives-Related Explosion: 27
  - Outside taxonomy: Hydrocarbon/vapour explosion in vessel (hot work): 2
  - Methane / Firedamp Explosion: 1
  - Outside taxonomy: Compressed gas cylinder explosion: 1

- No historical records exist for Coal-Dust, Combined Methane and Coal-Dust, Electrical or Mechanical Ignition, or Spontaneous Heating. For those situations the app shows synthetic scenarios and says no historical record matched.

## 3. Explosion-category classifier (trained on the 200 synthetic scenarios)

- Model: TF-IDF (1–2 grams, sublinear tf, English stop words) + Logistic Regression (class_weight=balanced), C = 2 chosen by 5-fold stratified CV on macro-F1.
- Synthetic-only model, 5-fold stratified cross-validation on synthetic data: accuracy **0.685**, macro-F1 **0.662** (table below).
- Deployed model is trained on 228 rows: 200 synthetic + 28 historical DGMS narratives (27 blasting, 1 methane), each tagged with its source.
- Deployed model, 5-fold out-of-fold predictions reported per source: synthetic accuracy **0.730** (macro-F1 0.717, n=200); historical accuracy **0.964** (n=28, effectively one class, so macro-F1 is not meaningful).
- Caveat: synthetic scenarios are short and template-like, so cross-validation on them is optimistic. No near-duplicate pairs above 0.85 string similarity were found, but no grouping information exists to rule out paraphrase leakage.

## 4. Explosion-evidence gate (decides whether to run explosion analysis)

- On the 610 historical narratives, using the project's core-explosion flag as truth: precision **0.794**, recall **0.871**.
- On the 200 synthetic scenarios the gate fires for 100.0% (text only, no DGMS signal). Missed: none.
- False positives (7): mostly Ground movement (2), Falls (other than falls of ground) (2), Electricity (1), Other causes (1). These are narratives that mention blasting, gas or fire without an explosion; the app still shows them correctly as hazard context.
- Missed explosion records (4): C-2017-46 (Blasting - blown-through shot), C-2018-34 (Blasting - flyrock/projectile), N-2021-20 (Blasting - flyrock/projectile), N-2021-22 (Blasting - flyrock/projectile).

## 5. Historical retrieval (leave-one-out over the 31 core explosion records)

- Plain text similarity over all 610 records: on average **54.3%** of the top 6 are explosion records (random baseline 4.9%).
- Explosion mode (explosion records only, +0.15 for the same mapped category): on average **88.2%** of the top 6 share the query record's category.

## 6. What the app does with each dataset

- Historical records: shown as accident cards and the ONLY evidence counted for preventive measures.
- Synthetic scenarios: train the explosion-category classifier and appear in a separate, labelled list. Never shown as accidents and never counted as evidence.
- The existing 8-group DGMS hazard classifier is unchanged.
